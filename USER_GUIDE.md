# CampusConnect User Guide

Welcome to CampusConnect — the student gig marketplace and professional networking platform.

---

## 🎓 For Students
### 1. Building your Profile
- Complete your profile under `/profile` by adding your college, graduation year, skills list, and avatar image.
- **Resume Analyzer:** Go to `/dashboard/student/resume-analyzer`, upload your PDF resume, and get dynamic AI scores and improvement tips.

### 2. Finding & Applying to Gigs
- Browse active listings at `/browse-gigs` or search specific categories.
- Click "Apply", draft your pitch, and set your bids.
- Use **Career Copilot** (`/dashboard/student/career-copilot`) to chat with our AI assistant for cover-letter revisions and mock-interview preparation.

---

## 💼 For Clients & Startups
### 1. Posting Gigs
- Click "Post Gig" in the Client Hub `/client-hub/post-gig`.
- Define the project title, description, skills tags, and budget.

### 2. Hiring & Escrow Locking
- Review applications under `/client-hub/applicants`.
- When hiring, you will be redirected to `/checkout` to lock the gig budget in our secure Escrow platform.
- Once completed, click "Release Payment" in the client dashboard to initiate the payout to the student.
