# Email DNS Configuration

CampusConnect currently does not have a verified email provider configuration (the codebase uses a placeholder for Resend/SMTP). 
As a result, we cannot programmatically verify DKIM or automate the creation of the exact SPF records.

Below are the exact manual steps to configure this when you provision an email provider (e.g., Resend).

## 1. SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
Currently, `campusconnectco.in` is missing an SPF record.
**Action:** Go to your DNS provider (e.g. Vercel, GoDaddy, Cloudflare) and add the following TXT record.
- **Type:** `TXT`
- **Name/Host:** `@` (or `campusconnectco.in`)
- **Value:** `v=spf1 include:sendgrid.net ~all` *(Replace `sendgrid.net` with `resend.com` or your actual provider)*.

## 2. DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
DKIM requires specific records generated by your email provider.
**Action:** 
1. Log into your email provider's dashboard (e.g., Resend).
2. Add your domain. They will provide a TXT or CNAME record.
3. Example for Resend: Add a TXT record with **Name/Host:** `resend._domainkey` and the provided public key as the **Value**.

## 3. DMARC
DMARC is already correctly configured to: `v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; adkim=r; aspf=r; rua=mailto:dmarc_rua@onsecureserver.net;`
No further action is required for DMARC.
