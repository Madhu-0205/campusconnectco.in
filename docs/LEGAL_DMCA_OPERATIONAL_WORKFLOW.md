# DMCA Operational Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

**Classification**: Internal Compliance Standard Operating Procedure  
**Applies to**: Engineering, Trust & Safety, and Legal Operations  
**Statutory Basis**: 17 U.S.C. § 512 (Digital Millennium Copyright Act)

> [!CAUTION]
> **Legal Disclaimer & Safe Harbor Requirement**:
> This internal SOP guides operational handling of copyright notices, takedowns, and counter-notices. The existence or adoption of this SOP does **not** independently establish or guarantee DMCA Safe Harbor immunity under 17 U.S.C. § 512(c). Safe Harbor requires fulfilling all statutory prerequisites, including maintaining active, verified Designated Agent registration with the U.S. Copyright Office Directory (renewed every 3 years).

---

## 1. Notice Intake & Logging

1. **Intake Channels**:
   - Notices arrive via `dmca@campusconnectco.in` or postal mail to the Designated Agent.
2. **Action**:
   - **Document the date of receipt and all required actions** immediately upon receipt in the internal compliance register.
   - Assign a unique Notice Tracking ID: `DMCA-YYYY-XXXX`.

---

## 2. Validating Statutory Elements (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3))

Review the notice to confirm presence of all 6 mandatory statutory elements:
- [ ] Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
- [ ] Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
- [ ] Identification of the infringing material and precise location/URL.
- [ ] Sufficient contact information (name, address, telephone, email).
- [ ] Statement of good-faith belief that use is unauthorized by law/owner.
- [ ] Statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and the complainant is authorized to act.

*If elements are defective*: Notify the complainant promptly of the specific missing statutory elements without waiving defenses.

---

## 3. Locating Material & Evidence Preservation

1. Locate the specific URL, gig listing, portfolio image, or resume asset.
2. **Preserve relevant records**:
   - Capture full-page screenshot and raw payload/content.
   - Record database row IDs, creator user ID, upload timestamps, and IP logs.
   - Store preserved evidence in the internal compliance evidence vault.

---

## 4. Disabling Access & User Notification

1. **Remove / Disable Access**:
   - Set status of the reported content to `DISABLED_COPYRIGHT` or remove from public visibility expeditiously.
2. **Notify Affected User**:
   - Transmit written notice to the user account email containing:
     - Identification of the removed content.
     - Explanation of the DMCA takedown claim.
     - Complete instructions on how to submit a statutory Counter-Notice pursuant to 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3).

---

## 5. Counter-Notice Processing & Restoration Workflow

1. **Intake of Counter-Notice**:
   - Confirm presence of required elements under 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(3) (signature, identification of removed material, penalty of perjury statement, jurisdiction consent).
2. **Forwarding to Complainant**:
   - Forward a copy of the counter-notice to the original complainant, informing them of the receipt of the counter-notification.
3. **Applicable Statutory Counter-Notice Waiting Period**:
   - Inform the complainant that access to the material will be restored within the **applicable statutory counter-notice waiting period**, unless the complainant notifies the Designated Agent that they have filed an action seeking a court order to restrain the subscriber from engaging in infringing activity.
4. **Restoration & Escalation**:
   - **Legal review required before restoration where appropriate**.
   - **Escalate ambiguous or disputed cases** to external legal counsel prior to restoring content.
   - If no court action notice is provided by the end of the applicable statutory counter-notice waiting period, access may be restored only in accordance with completed legal review.

---

## 6. Repeat Infringer Tracking (17 U.S.C. § 512(i))

1. Maintain an internal strike register linked to user IDs:
   - **Strike 1**: Formal warning + content removal.
   - **Strike 2**: 30-day suspension of posting/upload privileges.
   - **Strike 3**: Permanent account termination.
2. Ensure accounts terminated under the repeat infringer policy are blocked from re-registering using matching verified identity credentials or payment methods.

---

## 7. Designated Agent Maintenance & Copyright Office Renewals

1. Designated Agent details on `/dmca` must match official operational contacts.
2. **Copyright Office Directory Renewal Tracking**:
   - U.S. Copyright Office regulations require re-registering or renewing the designation in the online directory every **three (3) years**.
   - Maintain a recurring operational calendar alert 90 days before the renewal deadline to prevent lapses in registration.
